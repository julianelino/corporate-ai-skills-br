# Source Policy

Use `T1` authoritative sources for critical conclusions: public legislation, regulators, courts, accounting standards, official manuals, and valid corporate documents. `T2` is official technical material such as FAQs, layouts, and notes. `T3` professional material can explain practice but cannot replace T1/T2. `T4` benchmarks inform architecture only.

Record source identifier, access date, relevance, authority, effective date, and applicable jurisdiction. For a rule whose timing matters, record `effective_from`, `effective_until`, `last_verified`, `status`, `supersedes`, and `superseded_by`. Valid statuses are `CURRENT`, `SUPERSEDED`, `HISTORICAL`, `PENDING`, and `UNKNOWN`.

Never reproduce substantial paid, proprietary, or restricted content. Link, cite, or write an original allowed summary. Corporate documents remain private controlled context and must never be promoted to public knowledge.
