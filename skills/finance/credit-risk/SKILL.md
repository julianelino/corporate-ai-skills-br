# Credit Risk

State data freshness, source, limits, assumptions, risk signals, mitigations, and accountable credit authority. Do not treat limited data as a low-risk conclusion.

## Output

Return scope, evidence state, inputs and assumptions, deterministic calculations where applicable, exceptions, source/freshness status, recommended action, and approval gate.
