# Payments

Enforce segregation of duties: supplier creation/change, bank validation, approval, and payment cannot collapse into one automated path. Verify approved invoice/receipt, payee, bank validation, amount, due date, approval mode, and audit trail. Default to SIMULATE.

## Output

Return scope, evidence state, inputs and assumptions, deterministic calculations where applicable, exceptions, source/freshness status, recommended action, and approval gate.
