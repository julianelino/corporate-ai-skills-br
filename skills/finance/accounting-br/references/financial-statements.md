# Financial Statements (Demonstrações Contábeis)

## Topics Covered
- financial statements

## Purpose
Determine which financial statements an entity is required to present, their individual purpose, and how recognized/measured items (assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, expenses) roll up into each — before advising on or reviewing a set of financial statements.

## When to Load
Load whenever a request involves the complete set of financial statements — which ones are required for a given entity type, what each one presents, or how they interrelate — before assessing or drafting statement content.

## Scope
Covers CPC 26 (R1) (Apresentação das Demonstrações Contábeis) and CPC 09 (Demonstração do Valor Adicionado), alongside Lei nº 6.404/1976 and Lei nº 11.638/2007's statutory basis for the required statement set. Does not cover the recognition/measurement of the individual line items that populate these statements (see this skill's other references) or the specific accounting treatment of any one element.

## Core Concepts
- **The complete set (CPC 26 (R1))**: Balanço Patrimonial (BP), Demonstração do Resultado (DRE), Demonstração do Resultado Abrangente (DRA), Demonstração das Mutações do Patrimônio Líquido (DMPL), Demonstração dos Fluxos de Caixa (DFC), and notas explicativas. In Brazil, the Demonstração do Valor Adicionado (DVA, CPC 09) is added to this set, required for publicly-held companies under Lei 11.638/2007 and commonly required more broadly by Brazilian statutory practice.
- **Balanço Patrimonial (BP)**: the statement of financial position at a point in time — assets, liabilities, and equity, per the structure in `assets-liabilities-equity.md`.
- **Demonstração do Resultado (DRE)**: the income statement for a period — revenue, costs, and expenses per `revenue-expenses-cost.md`, arriving at net income/loss for the period.
- **Demonstração do Resultado Abrangente (DRA)**: extends the DRE with "other comprehensive income" items (e.g., certain revaluation and translation adjustments) that bypass the income statement but affect equity — required for all entities except PMEs (small/medium entities under the simplified framework), microempresas, and EPPs.
- **Demonstração das Mutações do Patrimônio Líquido (DMPL)**: reconciles the opening and closing balance of each equity component (capital social, reservas, ajustes de avaliação patrimonial, prejuízos acumulados) for the period, showing all movements (net income, dividends, capital transactions, reserve constitution).
- **Demonstração dos Fluxos de Caixa (DFC)**: presents cash inflows/outflows classified as operating, investing, and financing activities — a distinct view from the accrual-based DRE, reconciling accrual-basis results to actual cash movement.
- **Demonstração do Valor Adicionado (DVA, CPC 09)**: a Brazilian-specific statement showing the wealth generated by the entity and its distribution among employees, government, capital providers, and shareholders — not part of the core IFRS statement set, but a distinct Brazilian statutory/CPC requirement.
- **Applicability by entity size (per CPC 26/simplified frameworks)**: BP and DRE are required for essentially all entities; DRA, DMPL, DFC, and DVA carry different applicability depending on entity classification (full CPC framework vs. PME/micro/EPP simplified regimes) — do not assume the full six/seven-statement set applies uniformly to every entity without confirming its classification. `CORPORATE_CONTEXT_REQUIRED` for the specific entity's classification and applicable framework.

## Decision Points
1. What is the entity's classification (full CPC framework, PME, microempresa/EPP), and which specific statements are therefore required? `CORPORATE_CONTEXT_REQUIRED`.
2. Does the entity's structure/status (publicly-held vs. closely-held) trigger the DVA requirement specifically?
3. For a specific line item or balance, which statement(s) does it appear in, and are those presentations internally consistent (e.g., does the DMPL's closing patrimônio líquido reconcile to the BP's equity total)?
4. Does the DFC's classification of a specific cash flow (operating/investing/financing) correctly reflect the nature of the underlying activity, not merely its balance-sheet counterpart's classification?

## Required Facts
- The entity's size/type classification, to determine the applicable statement set. `CORPORATE_CONTEXT_REQUIRED`.
- Whether the entity is publicly-held (relevant to DRA/DVA applicability specifics).
- The reporting period and comparative-period requirements.

## Required Evidence
- The entity's classification documentation (revenue size, public/private status) supporting which framework applies. `CORPORATE_CONTEXT_REQUIRED`.
- The underlying recognized/measured balances (per this skill's other references) that populate each statement.

## Exceptions
- Microempresas and EPPs are generally exempt from DMPL, DFC, and DVA under the simplified frameworks — do not require these statements from an entity in that classification without confirming its actual current status.
- A first-time adopter of a given framework (or a framework transition) may have specific transitional presentation requirements not covered by this reference's steady-state framing.

## Risk Considerations
- Assuming the full six/seven-statement set applies to every entity regardless of size classification risks both over- and under-preparing required statements.
- Treating the DFC's cash-flow classification as automatically mirroring the DRE's accrual-basis classification of the same underlying transaction is a common error — the two statements answer different questions and can classify the same economic event differently.
- Presenting the DVA as an internationally-recognized IFRS statement (rather than a Brazilian-specific CPC 09 requirement) misstates its status when explaining Brazilian financial statements to an international audience.

## Human Escalation Conditions
Escalate for human accounting review whenever: an entity's size/framework classification is uncertain and affects which statements are required; a DFC classification is genuinely ambiguous between operating/investing/financing; or statement interconnections (BP-DMPL-DRE reconciliation) show an unexplained discrepancy.
