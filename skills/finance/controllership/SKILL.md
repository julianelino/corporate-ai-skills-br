# Controllership

Trace KPI and variance claims to controlled actuals, policies, periods, materiality, and owners. Delegate specialized accounting, tax, or FP&A determination.

## Output

Return scope, evidence state, inputs and assumptions, deterministic calculations where applicable, exceptions, source/freshness status, recommended action, and approval gate.
