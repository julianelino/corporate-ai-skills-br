# Billing Invoicing

Trace billed amount to approved contractual evidence and detect billing above ceiling, missing measurement, duplicate period, or unapproved adjustment. Separate mirror from issuance.

## Output

Return scope, evidence state, inputs and assumptions, deterministic calculations where applicable, exceptions, source/freshness status, recommended action, and approval gate.
