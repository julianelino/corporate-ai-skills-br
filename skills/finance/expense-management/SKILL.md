# Expense Management

Validate minimum evidence, period, owner, cost center, policy version, and duplicate indicators. Flag missing receipt or exception; preserve privacy and anti-fraud boundaries.

## Output

Return scope, evidence state, inputs and assumptions, deterministic calculations where applicable, exceptions, source/freshness status, recommended action, and approval gate.
