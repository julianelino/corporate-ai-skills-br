# Financial Fraud Risk

Apply anomaly ≠ fraud. Preserve facts, alternative explanations, data quality, evidence needs, confidentiality, and escalation. Route formal investigation to accountable functions.

## Output

Return scope, evidence state, inputs and assumptions, deterministic calculations where applicable, exceptions, source/freshness status, recommended action, and approval gate.
