# Corporate Risk

Classify the case from `R0` informational to `R6` critical and apply applicable tags. Consider legal, people, financial, accounting, tax, privacy, compliance, fraud, reputational, and security exposure. Increase verification and decrease autonomy as risk grows.

Separate confirmed, calculated, inferred, alleged, estimated, and unknown evidence. Identify missing facts, current-source needs, decision owner, and least-grave safe alternative. Map the outcome to `AUTO`, `REVIEW`, `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`, or `DUAL_APPROVAL`.

Return risk classification, evidence gaps, control requirements, escalation owner, approval gate, safe operating mode, and a concise rationale. Never approve, accuse, terminate, pay, or override segregation of duties.
