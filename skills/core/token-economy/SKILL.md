# Token Economy

For `/request`, inspect intent, necessary skills, references, tools, history, corporate context, and removable material. For `/audit` or `/refactor`, identify oversized cores, duplicate rules/sources, overlap, misplaced knowledge, over-routing, unnecessary web calls, and context leakage. For `/measure`, compare against contract budgets and assign `A+`, `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, or `F` with evidence.

Keep deep expertise in references and engines, not repeated in every `SKILL.md`. Never delete safety, legal, source, or approval information merely to reach a numeric target.
