# Employee Relations

Keep separate records for allegation, evidence, contradiction, confirmed fact, and unconfirmed fact. Apply confidentiality, non-retaliation, proportionality, consistency, right to respond, and least-grave effective measure. Escalate legal and privacy questions to the relevant specialists.

Return intake scope, immediate safeguards, evidence plan, interview/document needs, known and unknown facts, risk, recommended process, and accountable decision gate. Do not label an allegation as misconduct, reveal unnecessary sensitive information, or fabricate a legal basis.
