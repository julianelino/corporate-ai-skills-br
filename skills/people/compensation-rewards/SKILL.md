# Compensation Rewards

Establish approved job architecture, market evidence, performance evidence, internal comparators, policy, budget, and decision owner. Explain equity, affordability, legal/privacy constraints, and alternatives. Delegate payroll cost and FP&A impact.

## Output

Return scope, confirmed/calculated/inferred/alleged/estimated/unknown facts, required context, risks, recommendation or draft, and human approval/escalation boundary.
