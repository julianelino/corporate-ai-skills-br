# eSocial (Obrigações de Envio e Eventos de Folha)

## Topics Covered
- esocial

## Purpose
Determine which eSocial events a given payroll fact (remuneration, payment, termination) must generate, their statutory deadlines, and how they cross-check against DCTFWeb — before advising on or configuring an eSocial-reporting-linked payroll process.

## When to Load
Load whenever a request involves eSocial reporting obligations for payroll — which event applies to a given fact, its deadline, or how a reporting error/gap should be corrected — before advising on eSocial compliance or troubleshooting a rejected/pending event.

## Scope
Covers the periodic payroll-linked eSocial events (S-1200 remuneração, S-1210 pagamentos, S-1299 fechamento) and their DCTFWeb integration, under the current S-1.3 leiaute (in force since December 2024). Does not cover the specific non-periodic events (admissão, afastamento, etc.) in detail, or the broader SPED ecosystem beyond eSocial's payroll-linked events (see `sped-compliance` skill for ECD/ECF/EFD-Reinf specifically).

## Core Concepts
- **S-1200 (Remuneração)**: reports the calculated remuneration for each worker for the competência — the amounts, rubrics, and their classification (feeding from the `incidences.md` analysis of what each rubric compensates and how it should be classified).
- **S-1210 (Pagamentos de Rendimentos do Trabalho)**: reports the actual payment of amounts to the worker — distinct from S-1200's calculation-and-accrual reporting; under the current S-1.3 leiaute, S-1210 (together with S-2501 and S-5002) absorbed the reporting content that was previously carried by the now-discontinued DIRF (Declaração do Imposto de Renda Retido na Fonte) — a materially significant structural change from the pre-S-1.3 regime.
- **S-1299 (Fechamento dos Eventos Periódicos)**: closes the periodic-events window for the competência; its acceptance by eSocial finalizes the calculation bases for the period and enables the corresponding débitos to flow into DCTFWeb — until S-1299 is accepted, the competência's periodic reporting is not considered complete.
- **Deadline**: the periodic events (S-1200, S-1210, S-1299, and related) are commonly due by the 15th of the month following the competência (adjusted for non-business days) — verify the current exact deadline against `esocial`/`receita` guidance for the specific competência, since deadline mechanics can shift.
- **DCTFWeb cross-check**: DCTFWeb reconciles the débitos generated by the closed eSocial periodic events against CPFs, amounts, and incidência codes — a mismatch between the payroll's actual rubric classification (per `incidences.md`) and what was reported in S-1200/S-1210 will surface as a DCTFWeb inconsistency, not merely an eSocial-internal issue.
- **S-1.3 leiaute (current, since December 2024)**: the currently-applicable eSocial version; it replaced S-1.2, with the DIRF-absorption into S-1210/S-2501/S-5002 as its most significant payroll-relevant change — verify the current leiaute version before assuming any pre-2024 procedural detail still applies unchanged.

## Decision Points
1. Does this payroll fact require an S-1200 (calculation/accrual), an S-1210 (actual payment), or both — and are they being reported for the correct, matching competência?
2. Has S-1299 been accepted for the competência in question? If not, the periodic reporting cycle for that competência is incomplete and DCTFWeb integration has not yet occurred.
3. Does the rubric classification used in S-1200 match the `incidences.md` analysis of that rubric's true nature? A mismatch here is a likely source of a DCTFWeb inconsistency.
4. Is the deadline calculation (15th of the following month, adjusted for non-business days) being applied to the correct competência, and is it the currently-applicable deadline?
5. Since the amounts previously reported via DIRF now flow through S-1210/S-2501/S-5002 under S-1.3, has any legacy DIRF-era assumption been updated to reflect the current reporting path?

## Required Facts
- The competência and the specific payroll fact (remuneration calculation, payment, or termination) being reported.
- Whether S-1299 has been accepted for the relevant competência.
- The rubric classifications used in S-1200/S-1210, cross-checked against `incidences.md`.
- The current eSocial leiaute version in effect (S-1.3 as of this reference's research) and any subsequent version change. `CORPORATE_CONTEXT_REQUIRED` to confirm the company's current eSocial system/vendor version alignment.

## Required Evidence
- eSocial event submission and acceptance records (S-1200, S-1210, S-1299) for the competência in question.
- DCTFWeb reconciliation output, where a mismatch is being investigated.
- The payroll system's rubric-to-event mapping configuration. `CORPORATE_CONTEXT_REQUIRED`.

## Exceptions
- Non-periodic events (e.g., S-2200 admissão, S-2299 desligamento, S-2306/S-2399 for specific contract changes) are triggered by specific HR events rather than the monthly competência cycle — this reference covers only the periodic payroll-linked events; a termination's specific non-periodic reporting requirement is a related but distinct topic from the periodic S-1200/S-1210/S-1299 cycle described here.
- Small employers or specific simplified regimes may have different obligation thresholds or simplified reporting paths — `CORPORATE_CONTEXT_REQUIRED` to confirm the company's applicable regime before assuming the general obligation set described here applies unmodified.

## Risk Considerations
- A rubric misclassified in `incidences.md` terms but reported consistently (i.e., consistently wrong) in S-1200 will not necessarily trigger an immediate DCTFWeb mismatch, but does create latent tax/contribution exposure that a subsequent audit could surface — "no current system error" is not the same as "compliant."
- Assuming DIRF-era reporting logic still applies after the S-1.3 transition risks misconfiguring S-1210/S-2501/S-5002 reporting — this is a live, transition-related risk given how recently (December 2024) this change took effect.
- Missing the S-1299 closure step leaves the competência's periodic reporting incomplete even if S-1200/S-1210 were individually submitted — treat closure as a required, distinct final step, not an automatic consequence of submitting the earlier events.

## Human Escalation Conditions
Escalate for human payroll/tax review whenever: a DCTFWeb mismatch is identified and its root cause is unclear; the company's eSocial leiaute version alignment with the current S-1.3 requirements is uncertain; a rubric classification dispute (see `incidences.md`) has downstream eSocial reporting implications; or a competência's S-1299 closure is delayed past the applicable deadline.

## Freshness Requirements
Critical. The eSocial leiaute version (S-1.3 as of this reference's research, effective December 2024) and its specific event-field requirements are actively maintained and can change; re-verify the currently-applicable leiaute version and deadline before configuring or relying on a specific reporting behavior.

## Effective-Date Considerations
- **CURRENT (since December 2024):** eSocial leiaute S-1.3, under which S-1210/S-2501/S-5002 absorbed the reporting content previously carried by DIRF, which was discontinued.
- **SUPERSEDED (before December 2024):** leiaute S-1.2, under which DIRF remained a separate, required annual declaration alongside eSocial's periodic events.
- Any process documentation, training material, or system configuration still referencing a DIRF-filing step should be treated as potentially stale and re-verified against the current S-1.3 requirements.
