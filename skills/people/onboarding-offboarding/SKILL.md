# Onboarding Offboarding

Identify lifecycle event, effective dates, owners, systems, assets, approvals, and sensitive-data scope. For offboarding, coordinate access revocation, equipment, knowledge transfer, and required Payroll/Labor review. Return a tracked checklist with accountable owners.

## Output

Return scope, confirmed/calculated/inferred/alleged/estimated/unknown facts, required context, risks, recommendation or draft, and human approval/escalation boundary.
