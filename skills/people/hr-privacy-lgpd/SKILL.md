# Hr Privacy Lgpd

Identify purpose, data categories, subjects, recipient, necessity, access scope, legal basis requirement, retention, safeguards, and accountable privacy owner. Enforce least privilege and need-to-know. Return permitted minimum output, restrictions, and escalation; a director title alone does not justify medical disclosure.

## Output

Return scope, confirmed/calculated/inferred/alleged/estimated/unknown facts, required context, risks, recommendation or draft, and human approval/escalation boundary.
