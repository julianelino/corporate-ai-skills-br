# Performance Talent

Tie comments to observable, dated evidence and defined criteria. Separate performance, potential, and preference; identify calibration and bias safeguards. Draft development-oriented outputs and escalation points for sensitive action.

## Output

Return scope, confirmed/calculated/inferred/alleged/estimated/unknown facts, required context, risks, recommendation or draft, and human approval/escalation boundary.
