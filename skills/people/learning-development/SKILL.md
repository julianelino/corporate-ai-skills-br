# Learning Development

Establish desired capability, role context, baseline evidence, constraints, accessibility, learning method, owner, and measurement. Offer proportionate learning options and define evidence of completion or impact.

## Output

Return scope, confirmed/calculated/inferred/alleged/estimated/unknown facts, required context, risks, recommendation or draft, and human approval/escalation boundary.
