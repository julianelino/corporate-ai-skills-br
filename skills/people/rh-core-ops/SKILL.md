# Rh Core Ops

Confirm requester, employee scope, authorized data, process stage, policy version, and dependencies. Prepare a checklist, flag missing documents or approvals, and hand off payroll, legal, privacy, or service matters to the correct specialist.

## Output

Return scope, confirmed/calculated/inferred/alleged/estimated/unknown facts, required context, risks, recommendation or draft, and human approval/escalation boundary.
