# Talent Acquisition

Define job-relevant criteria before reviewing candidates. Separate supplied evidence from unknowns; lack of information is not a negative score. Produce a consistent scorecard, interview plan, comparison, and accountable decision gate.

## Output

Return scope, confirmed/calculated/inferred/alleged/estimated/unknown facts, required context, risks, recommendation or draft, and human approval/escalation boundary.
