# People Analytics

Apply `metric → baseline → deviation → segmentation → driver → impact → action`. Define population, period, denominator, source, missingness, and privacy controls. Distinguish observed drivers from inferences and publish aggregates where possible.

## Output

Return scope, confirmed/calculated/inferred/alleged/estimated/unknown facts, required context, risks, recommendation or draft, and human approval/escalation boundary.
