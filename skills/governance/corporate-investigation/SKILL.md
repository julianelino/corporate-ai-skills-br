# Corporate Investigation

Establish mandate, scope, allegation ledger, immediate safety/non-retaliation measures, preservation requirements, investigator authority, witnesses, evidence chain, timeline, and confidentiality controls. Keep allegation, evidence, contradiction, confirmed fact, unconfirmed fact, and unknown separate.

Return a proportional investigation plan, evidence matrix, interview topics, legal/privacy handoffs, findings limitations, and accountable decision path. Never coach evidence, make an accusation, or convert an anomaly into fraud.
