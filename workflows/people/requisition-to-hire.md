# Talent Acquisition: requisition-to-hire

## Flow

rh-business-partner → talent-acquisition → compensation-rewards → payroll-br → fpa → corporate-risk → human hiring decision

## Controls

Use job-relevant scorecard, approved requisition and budget; exclude protected attributes. Offer preparation is not approval.

Run in `SIMULATE` until all required approvals are present. Record requester, owner, evidence, decision, timestamp, and exceptions in the applicable controlled system.
